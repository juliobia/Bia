# Skylark: Deadline Run — dossier

## The game
An original 3D flight game for the desktop browser. You fly a small two-seat sport plane, *Skylark*, from the airfield on **Kestrel Cay**, a made-up volcanic island. Twelve beacon rings circle the island. Thread them in order before the deadline clock runs out (each ring adds 12 seconds), then fly home, land and stop on the runway.

Score = rings × 1000 + a win bonus + time left × 50 + a bonus for a soft touchdown (sink rate).

## How it plays
- **Take-off and landing for real:** throttle up, rotate at about 65 kt, keep the gear and wings level on touchdown. Landing off the runway, too hard or banked is a crash.
- **Two views:** a chase camera, and a cockpit with six lit gauges (airspeed, attitude, altimeter, RPM, heading, vertical speed).
- **HUD:** speed and altitude tapes, heading tape, pitch ladder, bank scale, flight-path marker, target diamond with distance, minimap, deadline timer, STALL and PULL UP warnings.
- **Time of day (T):** day, golden hour, night. At night the runway, approach and edge lights glow, the tower, hangars and village windows are lit, and the aircraft has a landing light, nav lights and strobes.
- **Weather (V):** clear, rain, storm. Rain brings overcast, fog and rain streaks with rain audio; storms add lightning that lights the scene and delayed thunder.
- **Wind and turbulence:** gusting crosswind drifts the aircraft (shown on the HUD and on a live windsock); turbulence grows with the weather and near the ground.
- **Cinematic replay:** after any flight, watch it again with chase, flyby, wing-cam and tower shots.
- **Ghost race (G):** your best winning run is saved locally and flown as a translucent ghost; every ring tells you how far ahead or behind it you are.
- **Smoke trail (X)** for airshow flying; mouse flying with pointer lock; touch controls on tablets.

## How it is built
- Plain WebGL2 and Canvas 2D, written from scratch: **no libraries, no assets, no network requests.** Everything (terrain, aircraft, buildings, trees, sounds) is generated by the code at load time.
- **Terrain:** a 320×320 heightfield (12.8 km) from value-noise fBm with ridged mountains, an island mask and a flattened airfield; coloured by height and slope (sand, grass, rock, snow) with per-pixel detail noise.
- **World:** ~4,000 conifer and broadleaf trees, ~650 boulders, two villages, a tower, three hangars, a windsock, two parked aircraft, a fully marked runway with lights, low-poly cumulus clouds, and an animated sea with Fresnel reflection and sun glint.
- **Aircraft:** a lofted fuselage, airfoil-section wings and tail, wheels in spats, a spinning propeller with a blur disc, nav lights and strobes.
- **Lighting:** a sky shader with sun disc and halo, three lighting presets, exponential fog, a landing-light cone, lightning flash, sun glare, lens flare, light glows and a vignette.
- **Flight model:** a lightweight arcade-sim: thrust, quadratic drag, gravity along the flight path, stall sink below about 50 kt, bank-to-turn, ground roll with brakes, wind drift and turbulence.
- **Audio:** WebAudio synthesis: an engine (sawtooth and square through a low-pass), wind and rain noise, ring chimes, warning beeps, an explosion, thunder.
- **Files:** `game/index.html` (menu and styles) and `game/game.js` (about 1,200 lines).

## Originality
The name, island, aircraft, art, sounds and rules are all original to this entry and generated in code. It is not based on any commercial game.

## Controls
R / Shift throttle up · F throttle down (or mouse wheel) · S / ↓ pull up · W / ↑ push down · A / D roll · Q / E rudder · B / Space brakes · C chase / cockpit · T time of day · V weather · G ghost · X smoke · P pause · M mute · click the view to fly with the mouse.
