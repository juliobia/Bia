DEADLINE RUN

Twelve beacon rings circle the island of Kestrel Cay. After the 3-2-1 countdown, take off and thread them in order before the deadline runs out (every ring buys extra seconds, a dead-centre pass scores PERFECT, and four optional gold rings pay a bonus), then bring the aircraft home and stop on the runway. Fly by day, golden hour or night, through rain and storms with crosswind, race the ghost of your best run, and watch a cinematic replay.
